# ♻️ KabadiKart – Your Scrap, Our Pickup

Making Scrap Selling Easier, Smarter, and More Convenient!

KabadiKart is a web-based platform designed to simplify the process of selling and managing household scrap. It connects users with scrap collection services through a simple and user-friendly interface.

Our goal is to make scrap management more accessible while encouraging recycling and environmental sustainability.

🚀 Features

- User Authentication:Login and Sign Up interface.
- Scrap Price List: View daily scrap prices per kilogram.
- Scrap Categories: Explore newspapers, cardboard, iron, plastic bottles, and e-waste.
- Quantity Estimator: Estimate scrap value based on quantity.
- Doorstep Pickup: Book scrap collection from your location.
- Digital Weighing: Information about accurate scrap measurement.
- User-Friendly Interface: Simple and modern website design.
- Responsive Design: Designed for a convenient browsing experience.

🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript

🎯 Project Objective

To provide a convenient digital platform where people can sell their scrap, estimate its value, and schedule doorstep pickups, promoting responsible waste management and recycling.
